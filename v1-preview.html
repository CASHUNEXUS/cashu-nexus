SAFETY GATE

Why receiving is not switched on yet

Receiving ecash can involve swapping proofs at a mint. Without durable encrypted proof storage, atomic state changes, duplicate-token protection, and crash recovery, a user could lose funds. Those safeguards must be designed and tested before enabling receive or send.

NEXT MILESTONES

What comes next

1. Design encrypted proof storage and recovery.
2. Add atomic proof-state handling and duplicate-token protection.
3. Build receive/send flows against an isolated test mint.
4. Add end-to-end tests, dependency auditing, a threat model, and independent security review.
