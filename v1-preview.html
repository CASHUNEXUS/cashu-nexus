PRODUCT UPDATE · NOVEMBER 2026
VERSION 1.0 · SIMULATED WALLET

A wallet demo
you can actually use.

Version 1 focuses on a complete interactive simulation: create a pretend transfer, receive pretend sats, watch the balance update, and inspect the activity history. Reviewers can explore freely without connecting to a mint or risking real funds.

Launch the demo ↗
CLEAR SAFETY BOUNDARY

100% simulated. 0 real payments.

No Bitcoin, Cashu token, Lightning invoice, private key, seed phrase, or real wallet connection is used. Balances and transfers are fictional and saved only in this browser's local storage. Reset the demo any time from Settings.

01 · BALANCE

Live demo balance

Watch your fictional satoshi balance change after every simulated send or receive.

02 · SEND

Simulated outgoing payment

Choose an amount, review it, and confirm. The demo prevents overspending.

03 · RECEIVE

Simulated incoming payment

Create a clearly fake request, then press Simulate Incoming Payment to add demo sats.

04 · ACTIVITY

Transaction history

Review incoming and outgoing demo transactions, filter or search them, and export CSV.

WHAT THIS IS NOT

This is not a production wallet and must never be used to send, receive, store, or claim real funds. The separate experimental Cashu SDK branch is not part of this public demo and is not represented as a completed wallet.
