KNOW WHAT YOU HOLD

Privacy & safety.

Good privacy starts with informed choices.

◉
01 · PRIVACY MODEL

Chaumian ecash

Cashu uses blind signatures. In simplified terms, this helps prevent a mint from linking the exact ecash token it issued to the later spending of that token.

REDUCED LINKABILITY
◈
02 · ISSUER RISK

Mint trust

Cashu is a protocol, not a guarantee that every mint is honest, solvent or always available. A mint may go offline, censor redemptions or lose funds.

YOU CHOOSE THE ISSUER
⌑
03 · TOKEN SAFETY

Bearer instruments

Anyone who obtains a valid ecash token may be able to spend it. Treat tokens like cash: do not post them, screenshot them or share them with support agents.

KEEP TOKENS PRIVATE
⛨
04 · RECOVERY

Backups matter

Understand the wallet's recovery model before depositing value. A backup strategy is not universal across mints or wallet implementations.

PLAN BEFORE DEPOSITING
Prototype disclaimer

Cashu Nexus is an interface concept. It does not create or store keys, proofs, tokens, invoices or payment requests; it does not connect to a mint and must not be used with real funds.

A QUICK NOTE

A wallet experience concept.

Cashu Nexus is an interactive front-end prototype designed to demonstrate a clear, privacy-aware ecash wallet experience.

What works: navigation, local demo balance, sample activity, mint bookmarks, CSV export and responsive layouts.

What does not: real Cashu token parsing, mint connections, Lightning invoices, proof management, key storage or payments.
